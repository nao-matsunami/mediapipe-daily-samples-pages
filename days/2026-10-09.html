Notable example and why it matters

Today's notable point is to treat an official release such as MediaPipe v1.1.0 as a trigger for small Web Task checks rather than immediately shipping it into a live experience. The checks are bundle import, WASM fileset, model asset, delegate fallback, and task options. The official demo is used as a parity reference, not as the center of the archive.

制作に使えそうな技術メモ

Production note

実装では `createFromOptions()` などの Task 初期化を直接画面体験へつながず、bundle import、`FilesetResolver`、model asset、delegate、Task options、公式 demo parity の順で smoke result を集めます。差分が大きい日は新機能を広げず、version pin と rollback path を先に確認してから公開します。

In production, do not connect task initialization such as `createFromOptions()` directly to the experience. First collect smoke results for bundle import, `FilesetResolver`, model assets, delegates, task options, and official demo parity. When release deltas are large, pin versions and confirm rollback paths before expanding features.
